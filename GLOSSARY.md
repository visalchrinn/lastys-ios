# Lastys

Lastys lets a person prove they are single and keep one confirmed relationship at a time. Trustee is the authority that holds the truth about who is linked to whom.

## Language

### Identity

**Lastys ID**:
A person's single identity on Lastys, held on one phone at a time and shown as a Personal ID such as `LT-8K2F-91QA`.
_Avoid_: Account, user, profile

### Relationships

**Relationship**:
A link between exactly two Lastys IDs, confirmed by both. A Lastys ID can have at most one active Relationship.
_Avoid_: Link, couple, match

**Partner**:
The other Lastys ID in your active Relationship.
_Avoid_: Linked person, match

**Taken**:
A display label meaning the Lastys ID has an active Relationship. It is not a domain state of its own.

**Verified Single**:
A display label meaning the Lastys ID has no active Relationship. It is not a domain state of its own.

**Verified Single card**:
A shareable image with your nickname, photo and Verified Single status as Trustee confirmed it at the moment the card was made, plus a Lastys QR so the viewer can check it live. It is a claim as of that moment, not a lasting proof. Its Lastys QR keeps working until the owner revokes it.
_Avoid_: Certificate, badge

**Quick hide**:
Hides the status card and Shared Moments on your own phone's screen. It never changes what a Scan of you shows.
_Avoid_: Privacy mode, hide status

**Relationship request**:
An offer to form a Relationship, sent by one Lastys ID to another straight after a Scan or through an Invite link. It becomes a Relationship only when the receiver accepts.
_Avoid_: Link request, invite

**Invite link**:
A link that carries a Relationship request to someone you haven't Scanned, who may not have Lastys yet. Only one person can use it, and it stops working after 7 days or when its sender cancels it.
_Avoid_: Invite code, referral

**End request**:
A Partner's request to end the Relationship, which the other Partner can agree to or decline.
_Avoid_: Breakup, unlink

**Team review**:
The Lastys team's decision on an End request that was declined or left unanswered for 7 days, or on a safety concern.
_Avoid_: Admin end, appeal

**Cooling-off period**:
The 7 days after a Relationship ends during which neither former Partner can send or accept a Relationship request.

### Scanning

**Lastys QR**:
A code that a Lastys ID shows, on its phone or on a Verified Single card, so someone can Scan it. It stays the same, and works offline, until its owner presses *Revoke*; then every older copy, such as a screenshot or a shared card, stops working and the owner gets a new one.
_Avoid_: Barcode, share code

**Revoke**:
The owner retires their current Lastys QR and gets a new one. Needs internet, because Trustee is what stops accepting the old QR.
_Avoid_: Expire, reset

**Scan**:
One reading of someone's Lastys QR, by camera or from a photo; both count the same. It shows that person's status and whatever they chose to share. The scanned person sees the Scan only as an anonymous, time-only entry.
_Avoid_: Check, lookup

**Share level**:
What a Scan of your Lastys QR shows: *Status only* (the default) or *Status + history*.

**Relationship history**:
How many past Relationships a Lastys ID has had, each with only its start and end month. It never names past Partners.
_Avoid_: Dating history, past partners

### Moments

**Moment**:
A dated event that one Lastys ID owns, such as a birthday or first date. Lastys sends reminders before it, and it can repeat every year by the Khmer lunar date.
_Avoid_: Event, memory, anniversary (an anniversary is one type of Moment)

**Shared Moment**:
A Moment its owner has shared into their Relationship so the Partner sees it too. When the Relationship ends, each former Partner keeps or deletes their own copy.

**Moment card**:
A shareable image of one Moment, made by its owner. It never shows the Moment's note, a Lastys QR, or whether anyone is single or taken.
_Avoid_: Moment post, memory card

### Phones

**Phone transfer**:
Moving a Lastys ID from a working phone to a new one by scanning a transfer code. At most one every 30 days.
_Avoid_: Migration, device change

**Recovery code**:
A secret given to the person when the Lastys ID is created. On a new phone it starts a Recovery, which the current phone can cancel.
_Avoid_: Backup code, password

**Recovery**:
Moving a Lastys ID to a new phone with the Recovery code when the old phone is lost. It takes effect after a 7-day wait.
