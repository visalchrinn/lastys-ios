# Invite links let a Relationship form without a Scan

Until now, a Relationship request could only be sent straight after a Scan, so two people had to be together, phone to phone, before they could be linked. The Invite link lets a Lastys ID send a Relationship request to someone it has never Scanned, who may not even have Lastys yet. We accept that because a partner who isn't on Lastys can't show a Lastys QR, and making them install, onboard and meet up before the first link is a lot to ask.

The Invite link is a Relationship request delivered by link, not a new kind of link between people. Every rule of a Relationship request still holds: one active Relationship per Lastys ID, no sending or accepting during a Cooling-off period, Trustee confirms both sides, and the receiver must accept. To keep the loss of the in-person check small, the link works for one person only, expires after 7 days, can be cancelled by its sender, and stops working the moment its sender's Relationship forms some other way. A sender can have only one waiting Invite link.

## Considered Options

- **Scan only**: rejected because the partner has to be on Lastys and in the same place first.
- **Invite link that only installs the app, then requires a Scan**: rejected because it adds a meeting step without adding proof. Both people still confirm on their own phones either way.

## Consequences

- A Relationship no longer proves that the two people were ever in the same place. Scan results show only status and Relationship history, so nothing on screen claims otherwise.
- Invite links that have already been shared can't be recalled once real links ship, so the one-person, 7-day and cancel rules must be enforced by Trustee, not the app.
