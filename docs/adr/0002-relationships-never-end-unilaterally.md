# Relationships never end unilaterally

A Relationship ends only when both Partners agree, or when the Lastys team ends it after a Team review. That review is available if the other Partner declines the End request or doesn't answer within 7 days. A one-sided end would let someone reset to Verified Single in seconds and empty the Taken label of meaning. The cost is that a Partner can stall an ending. Quick hide, plus a 24-hour Team review for safety concerns, exists to stop that cost from trapping someone in an abusive Relationship.

## Consequences

- *Delete my data* is blocked while a Relationship is active. Otherwise a Partner could delete their Lastys ID to get out, which would be a one-sided end. The screen sends them to the End request and Team review instead.
